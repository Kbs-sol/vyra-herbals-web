# Guide: Activating Your Native WhatsApp Storefront

Your website's backend has a powerful, hidden feature already built into it: a fully automated **WhatsApp Ordering Chatbot**. 

When activated, a "Store" icon will appear directly on your WhatsApp Business profile. Customers can browse your products, add them to their cart, and checkout entirely within WhatsApp. As soon as a customer sends their cart, your automated system will instantly reply, create a draft order in your database, ask for their delivery address, and send them a secure Easebuzz payment link.

To activate this experience for your customers, you just need to configure your product catalog on Meta's servers. Here is the step-by-step guide to doing that:

---

### Phase 1: Create a Meta Product Catalog
Because WhatsApp is owned by Meta (Facebook), your WhatsApp products are managed through the Meta Commerce Manager.

1. Go to your **Meta Business Settings** (business.facebook.com/settings).
2. On the left-hand menu, scroll down to **Data Sources** and click on **Catalogs**.
3. Click the blue **Add** button and select **Create a New Catalog**.
4. Give it a name (e.g., "Vyra Herbals Catalog") and select **E-commerce** as the type.
5. Click **Create** and ensure your account is assigned full permissions to manage the catalog.

### Phase 2: Add Your Products
*Important: The product names and prices you enter here should match what you sell on your website so the chatbot calculates the total accurately.*

1. Once the catalog is created, click **Open in Commerce Manager** (in the top right corner).
2. On the left menu, click **Catalog** > **Items**.
3. Click the green **Add Items** button.
4. Select **Manual** (if you only have a few products) or **Data Feed** (if you want to upload an Excel sheet).
5. For each product, fill in:
   * **Image:** A clear picture of your herbal product.
   * **Title:** The product name (e.g., "Vyra Hair Oil").
   * **Description:** A short description of the benefits.
   * **Price:** The price in INR (Rs).
   * **Condition:** New.
6. Click **Save** for each item. 

### Phase 3: Link the Catalog to Your WhatsApp Number
Now that Meta knows what you sell, you need to tell WhatsApp to display it on your phone number.

1. Go to your **WhatsApp Manager** (from the Meta Business Suite).
2. On the left menu, look for **Account Tools** and click on **Catalog**.
3. You will see a dropdown menu asking you to choose a catalog. Select the "Vyra Herbals Catalog" you just created.
4. Click **Connect**.

### Phase 4: What Happens Next?
You are done! Within a few hours, Meta will approve your products. Here is exactly what will happen automatically:

1. **The Shop Icon:** A storefront icon will magically appear on your WhatsApp profile.
2. **Customer Adds to Cart:** A customer taps the icon, views your products, adds them to their cart, and taps "Send to Business".
3. **The Chatbot Takes Over:** The moment their cart hits your WhatsApp, the software we built takes over:
   * It instantly calculates their total and saves the items to your secure database as a "Draft Order".
   * It replies automatically: *"Great! Your order total is Rs. X. Please type your full delivery address (including Name, Street, City, State, and Pincode) to proceed."*
   * Once they type their address, it replies with an automated Payment Link for them to checkout securely, along with a button that says *"✅ I have paid"*.

You don't have to lift a finger until the payment is confirmed and it's time to ship!
